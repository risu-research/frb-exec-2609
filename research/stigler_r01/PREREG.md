# Stigler 2027 — R01 pre-analysis record (2026-11-23)

## Objective
Attempt to falsify the thesis that **holding the exact same AI companion functionality and user-facing risk constant, the corporate development/distribution architecture can change statutory compliance responsibility**. Compare this with the alternative hypothesis that these differences are already well documented, are intended risk targeting, or are legally too ambiguous to support an economic-relevance claim.

## Scope fixed before R01 run
Primary originals: Hawaii SB3001 introduced, SD1, SD2, HD1, HD2, HD3, CD1 (signed Act 248); Iowa SF2417 enrolled; Georgia SB540 signed; Oregon SB1546 chapter 85. Prior R00 primary text is frozen. Capture every HI amended version (official HTML) with SHA256 and explicit distinction between missing-document and missing-clause.

Three independent units:
1. **Product scope**: P1 full 3-feature companion, P2 conversational assistant missing unprompted emotion-based questions, P3 full companion embedded within a nonconversational app, P4 same full companion accessed by unregistered guest, P5 full feature set in enterprise productivity branding, P6 conversational general assistant missing memory/unprompted questions, P7 exact two-of-three companion requirements; each defined explicitly in source.
2. **Responsible actor**: A1 vertically integrated service; A2 distinct creator who builds a bot but neither hosts nor contracts with end users; A3 platform that exposes a third-party AI bot but did not build the chatbot; A4 foundation-model developer whose model powers another company's bot; A5 parent firm owning the subsidiary that develops and distributes bot, but not the direct product developer. These are controlled **hypothetical legal arrangements**, not actual company legal classifications.
3. **Enforcement**: general crisis-referral obligations, minor-specific reminders, annual reporting, public/private enforcement. Do not equate different deadlines or private remedies with market harm.

Precommitted 4 statuses:
- CLEAR-IN (only when all explicit text conditions affirmatively satisfied)
- CLEAR-OUT (only for directly stated exclusion or unsatisfied necessary criterion)
- AMBIGUOUS (legal fact-dependent, multiple plausible statutory interpretations)
- N/A (different duty/actor or no applicable scope)
Evidence for every manually adjudicated clause must include official URL and section. No statistical estimand or causal market result will be calculated using this descriptive table.

## Adversarial checks
- 'develops AND makes available' is **not** proof of universal immunity for firms separating roles: indirect offering/agency may count.
- Three-conjunct companion definitions are **risk-selection policy choices**; do not call exclusions evidence of capture.
- No actual company assumed covered. Poe's third-party bot architecture is independently documented but features needed for the statutory companion test are not publicly established.
- The 2026 FPF state-law mapping already reports scope patchwork, and Lancieri/Edelson/Bechtold (2025) analyze regulatory arbitrage; mere mapping is NOT a novel Stigler result.
- Timeline + testimony cannot prove lobbying causation.
- The official Hawaii conference report may call some changes 'technical, nonsubstantive'; test text and plausible legal effect separately, never override legislative characterization with author certainty.
- Exact state legal text might be scanned; unavailable OCR/text MUST remain unknown.

## R01 gate to proceed
1. Clearly material responsibility asymmetry for controlled identical product architecture **in at least two enacted jurisdictions**, robust to reasonable adverse legal readings.
2. Same result applies to real, documented supply chains (not merely invented toy roles) with fully documented matching 3-feature definitions.
3. New measurable market consequence, credible competition mechanism, or traceable stakeholder-to-amendment documentary chain.
If only (1) is possible and (2)-(3) not, record NO-GO for the original Stigler full paper and preserve observations for future legal scholarship.

This is an internal reproducibility and falsification memo, not a law firm opinion or final legal interpretation.
